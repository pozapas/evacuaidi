# Chapter 2 Resources

Supplementary resources for Chapter 2 of the dissertation, published as
*Who Leads, Who Follows? A Review of Leadership and Guidance Dynamics in
Evacuation*.

The review covers 265 studies identified through a primary database search and
a supplementary targeted search. The files below let the search, the screening
yield, the quality appraisal, and every figure in the paper be reproduced from
the underlying record.

## Files

### `search/`

- `search_query_log.pdf`: Query log for both search arms, with sources, dates, year spans, and yields.
- `supplementary_search_queries.csv`: The five supplementary keyword families as issued, with hit and retrieval counts.

### `corpus/`

- `included_studies.csv`: The 265 included studies with year, publication type, venue, DOI, and title. Composition is 195 journal articles, 61 conference papers, 4 book chapters, 3 preprints, and 2 doctoral dissertations.

### `screening/`

- `supplementary_arm_included.csv`: The 29 studies contributed by the supplementary targeted search.
- `supplementary_arm_included.bib`: Bibliographic records for the same 29 studies.

### `appraisal/`

- `appraisal_rubric.md`: The two appraisal instruments and the anchors applied to each criterion. Instrument A adapts the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool, version 2018; instrument B operationalizes established verification and validation criteria for evacuation models.
- `appraisal_by_study.csv`: The rating of every appraised study on every criterion.
- `appraisal_by_study.pdf`: The same record, typeset.
- `consistency_check.md`: Ten studies scored a second time independently, with the agreement figure.

The appraisal covers 223 studies. The simulation instrument was applied to 189
and the empirical instrument to 54; 26 studies report both designs and are
appraised on both. Criterion-level frequencies are computed over the studies to
which each criterion applies, which is 186 and 52 respectively.

### `bibliometrics/`

- `abstracts_corpus.txt`: The corpus text used for term co-occurrence analysis.
- `vosviewer_thesaurus.txt`: Term merge and deletion rules.
- `vosviewer_map.txt`, `vosviewer_network.txt`: The exported map and network, which can be reopened directly in VOSviewer.

Settings: VOSviewer 1.6.21, term co-occurrence over titles and abstracts,
binary counting, minimum 5 occurrences, top 60 percent by relevance,
association strength normalization.

### `figures/`

- `prisma_flow.drawio`, `prisma_flow.png`: Editable source and rendered version of the study selection diagram.
- `figure_data/`: The counts behind the publication-year, journal-frequency, appraisal, and modeling-approach figures.

### `prisma/`

- `prisma_2020_checklist.pdf`: Completed PRISMA 2020 checklist with the location of each item.
